## 🌱 Why VocTrainer Exists

Language learners rarely fail because of a lack of ambition. They falter because vocabulary practice is usually unstructured, unmotivated work that offers little feedback. VocTrainer was built on a simple bet: if learning vocabulary feels like being coached rather than being tested, learners stay longer, remember more, and enjoy the process instead of dreading it.

Think of VocTrainer as a friendly sports coach for your lexicon. It studies your rhythm, your weak spots, and your strongest areas, then designs practice sessions tailored to the person you are today — not the person you were three chapters ago.

---

## ✨ What Makes It Different

- **Coaching, not quizzing.** Every session gives corrective, encouraging feedback rather than a simple right-or-wrong stamp.
- **Adaptive difficulty.** Vocabulary difficulty rises and falls with your demonstrated performance, keeping you in the productive "sweet spot."
- **Context-first design.** Words are learned in sentences, stories, and dialogues — never as isolated lines on a card.
- **Progress you can visualize.** Streaks, retention curves, and goal tracking transform invisible effort into visible momentum.
- **A calm, distraction-light interface.** No flashing reward loops; instead, a steady, pleasant coach-like experience.

## 🏋️ The Coaching Engine

The heart of VocTrainer is its coaching engine, which behaves less like a grader and more like a mentor:

1. **Warm-up** — A short review of previously learned material gently wakes up your memory.
2. **Focus block** — New vocabulary is introduced in context, then tested in a few ways: recognition, recall, and application.
3. **Correction** — Mistakes are explained, not merely marked. The engine notes patterns.
4. **Cool-down** — The session ends with a quick recap to reinforce retention.
5. **Follow-up** — The engine schedules the next appearance of each word based on your personal forgetting curve.

This loop repeats with data-driven adjustments, making each session slightly better calibrated than the last.
